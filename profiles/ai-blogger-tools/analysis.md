# Evaluation goal
Evaluate this item for an AI design learner and creator selecting useful tools, image/video/design workflows, and automation.

# Weighted criteria
- News importance and likely impact: 35%.
- Concrete value as an article or short-video topic: 30%.
- Novelty relative to common AI coverage: 20%.
- Source credibility and supporting evidence: 15%.

# Scoring rubric
- 9-10: Essential and time-sensitive; strong evidence, major impact, and a distinctive creator angle.
- 7-8: High value; credible, concrete, and worth covering soon.
- 5-6: Useful background or incremental news, but not a priority today.
- 3-4: Shallow, repetitive, weakly supported, or mainly promotional.
- 0-2: Spam, rumor without evidence, clickbait, off-topic, or duplicated noise.

# Evaluation guidance
Do not reward popularity alone. Penalize copied announcements, vague predictions, affiliate content, unsupported benchmarks, and titles that exaggerate the source. Return a score based on the supplied evidence.
